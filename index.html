Stream Overview

Clean Waterways

Healthy streams support native aquatic life, macroinvertebrates, and local bird species across Auckland.

Urban Runoff

Litter, oil from roads, and heavy sediment wash into local streams during rain, harming aquatic ecosystems.

Our Mission

Teaching MRGS students Kaitiakitanga (guardianship) to inspect, test, and protect our local stream environments.

Testing Guide

pH Levels & Temp: Water that is too acidic or warm reduces oxygen and stresses fish and macroinvertebrate populations.

Clarity & Bugs: Turbid, muddy water blocks sunlight while macroinvertebrate counts reveal long-term stream health.
