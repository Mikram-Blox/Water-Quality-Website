Water Quality

MOUNT ROSKILL GRAMMAR SCHOOL • PROTECT OUR STREAMS

Stream Overview

Clean Waterways

Protect native life by planting streamside trees to create cooling shade, filtering water naturally, and keeping local habitats safe and undisturbed.

Urban Runoff

Stop pollution at the source by disposing of litter properly, washing cars on lawns instead of driveways, and preventing oils or harsh chemicals from entering stormwater drains.

Our Mission

Empowering MRGS students to step up as active caretakers, hands-on cleaning, and defending our local streams so they flourish for future generations.

Testing Guide

pH Levels & Temp: Keep the water cool and chemically stable by minimizing garden chemical use in the neighborhood and establishing thick native vegetation walls along the banks.

Clarity & Bugs: Reduce mud erosion by anchoring loose soil with native plants, and remove rubbish before it breaks down, ensuring a sunlit, clean environment where stream creatures can thrive.
