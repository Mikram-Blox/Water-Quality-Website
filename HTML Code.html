The Crisis

THE IMPACT OF LOGGING: Large-scale industrial logging and land clearing for cattle ranching destroy millions of hectares of trees every single year. Forests act as the earth's natural lungs, and losing them means less carbon dioxide is filtered out of our atmosphere, heavily speeding up global warming.

LOSS OF BIODIVERSITY: Rainforests are home to over 80% of the world's land-living species. When habitats are fragmented or completely flattened by heavy machinery, rare animals and plants lose their homes, pushing many vulnerable species toward complete extinction.

How to Help

SUSTAINABLE SHOPPING: You can make a massive difference just by checking labels at the grocery store. Look for FSC-certified paper products, avoid items containing unsustainable palm oil, and switch to digital notes instead of printing or using disposable notebooks whenever possible.

SUPPORTING REFORESTATION PROJECTS: Planting trees is one of the best ways to restore broken ecosystems. You can support local tree-planting charities, use eco-friendly search engines like Ecosia that plant trees while you browse, or volunteer for native bush regeneration projects in your local area.

About Us

WHO WE ARE: We are a group of student environmental advocates dedicated to raising awareness about forest conservation. This website was built as part of a computer science project to show how digital media and clean web layouts can spread vital environmental advisories directly to young people.

OUR FUTURE VISION: We hope to expand this platform into a school-wide environmental tracker network. By combining technology with real-world conservation data, we believe our generation can actively monitor local canopy loss and collaborate on community tree-planting goals to build a greener future.
